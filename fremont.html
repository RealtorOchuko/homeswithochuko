Living in Fremont

Why people choose Fremont.

  • School-driven demand

    Mission San Jose and nearby attendance areas are among the most requested in the Bay Area.

  • Commuter-friendly

    Fremont and Warm Springs BART, I-880 and I-680, plus the Dumbarton Bridge to the Peninsula.

  • Outdoors nearby

    Mission Peak, Lake Elizabeth, Coyote Hills and Niles Canyon for hiking and weekends.

  • Diverse food scene

    Some of the Bay Area's best Indian, Afghan and East Asian restaurants, all close to home.

Fremont FAQ

Common questions.

Also exploreSan FranciscoThe PeninsulaOaklandSan Jose
What do homes cost in Fremont?

Townhomes and condos often start near $800K, single-family homes in most neighborhoods run roughly $1.5M to $2.5M, and Mission San Jose homes can exceed $3M. I'll pull current comps for you.

Which Fremont schools are most in demand?

Schools in the Mission San Jose area are especially sought after, but several other attendance areas are highly rated too. Always confirm boundaries with Fremont Unified, since they can change.

How is the commute from Fremont?

BART reaches Oakland and San Francisco, while I-880, I-680 and the Dumbarton Bridge lead to Silicon Valley and the Peninsula. Commute times depend on traffic, so we'll test your route.
