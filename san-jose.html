Living in San Jose

Why people choose San Jose.

  • Close to employers

    Major tech campuses in San Jose, Santa Clara, Sunnyvale and Cupertino are a short drive away.

  • Connected

    SJC airport, Caltrain, VTA light rail and Highways 101, 280, 880 and 87.

  • Neighborhood variety

    From walkable Willow Glen to the hillside lots of Almaden and Silver Creek.

  • Sunny and outdoorsy

    Warm weather, Alum Rock Park, the Los Gatos Creek Trail and the Almaden Quicksilver trails.

For investors

Running the numbers in San Jose.

General notes, not legal or tax advice. Rules change, so we'll confirm specifics for each property.

Rent ordinance

San Jose's Apartment Rent Ordinance covers many apartments in 3+ unit buildings built before late 1979. Single-family homes follow state rules instead.

Townhomes & condos

Lower entry prices and steady tenant demand from nearby employers. Review HOA reserves and rules carefully.

ADUs & lot splits

California's ADU and SB 9 rules open up added-value options on many single-family lots.

San Jose FAQ

Common questions.

What do homes cost in San Jose?

Condos and townhomes can start around $600K to $1M, most single-family homes fall roughly between $1.3M and $2.5M, and neighborhoods like Almaden Valley and Willow Glen often run higher. I'll pull current comps for you.

Which San Jose areas have the best schools?

It depends on the district. Parts of west San Jose fall in Cupertino and Fremont Union districts, while Almaden and Willow Glen are served by San Jose Unified schools. We'll confirm assignments for every address.

Is San Jose good for first-time buyers?

It offers the widest range of price points in the South Bay, from condos and townhomes to starter single-family homes. I'll help you weigh location, HOA costs and long-term value.
