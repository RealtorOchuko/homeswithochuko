The Easy-Exit Listing Agreement

Not happy? Walk away.

Most listing agreements lock you in for months. Mine doesn't. I'd rather earn your business every week than hold you to a contract.

  1. Cancel with written notice anytime before you accept an offer.
  2. No cancellation fee, and no charge for the marketing I've already paid for.
  3. Staging I've advanced is reimbursed, at close or if you cancel.
  4. If you sell to a buyer who came through my marketing during the protection period, the agreed commission still applies.

Terms are set out in a written addendum to the listing agreement with Mosaik Real Estate. Ask me for a copy.

Seller FAQ

Good questions.

Anything else? Text me at (510) 552-9481.

What does the Sell-Smart package cost?

It's included in my listing commission. I pay for the inspection, photos, video, Zillow Showcase and marketing up front. Staging is the one item I advance and you reimburse at close.

Can I really cancel?

Yes. If you're not satisfied with my service, you can cancel with written notice before you accept an offer, with no cancellation fee. The full terms are in a written addendum to your listing agreement.

Why inspect before listing? Won't it find problems?

Buyers will find them anyway, in escrow, when they have leverage. Finding them first lets us decide what to fix, what to disclose and how to price, on your terms.

Do I have to stage?

No. We'll look at what staging costs against what it can add. Sometimes it's only the main rooms, or just decluttering and paint.

Which areas do you cover?

San Francisco, the Peninsula, Oakland, Fremont, Newark, San Jose and the surrounding towns.
