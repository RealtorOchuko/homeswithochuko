Anything else? Text me at (510) 552-9481.

Is it really free?

Yes. There's no cost and no obligation to list with me. Many homeowners request one every year or two just to track their equity.

How is this different from a Zestimate?

Automated estimates use public records and broad averages. I look at the specific homes buyers will compare yours to, factor in condition and upgrades, and apply what I know about how each neighborhood is selling right now.

Do you need to see the home?

Not for the first report. If you're thinking about selling, a short walkthrough lets me fine-tune the price and point out which preparation is worth the money.

Which areas do you cover?

San Francisco, the Peninsula, Oakland, Fremont, San Jose and the surrounding towns. If your home is farther out, I'll still help or connect you with a trusted local agent.

Will my information be shared?

No. Your details are used only to prepare your valuation and follow up with you. I never sell your information.
