FAQ

Good questions.

Don't see yours? Send it over. I'm happy to answer, even if you're not ready to move.

Which areas do you cover?

San Francisco, the Peninsula, Oakland, Fremont and San Jose, along with the towns around them. For anything farther out, I can refer you to a trusted local agent.

How do buyer agent fees work now?

Since 2024, buyers sign a written agreement with their agent before touring, and that agreement spells out compensation. Sometimes the seller covers it, sometimes it's negotiated into the offer. I'll walk you through every option up front so there are no surprises.

Do homes here really sell over asking?

It depends heavily on the neighborhood, price point and season. Some listings are priced to draw multiple offers, others are priced near value. I'll show you recent comps so you know what a home is actually likely to sell for.

How are property taxes calculated in California?

Under Prop 13, the base tax is about 1% of your purchase price, plus local bonds and assessments, which vary by city. Increases on the assessed value are capped each year. I'll estimate the full tax bill for any home you're considering.

Do you work with investors?

Yes. I help with single-family rentals, small multifamily, ADU potential and value-add properties, and I model rents, expenses and local rent rules before you write an offer.

Can I buy if I'm relocating from out of state?

Absolutely. Many of my clients start remotely with live video tours, digital disclosures and e-signing, then visit for a short trip to see their top picks.

What does a home valuation include?

A CMA comparing your home with recent nearby sales, active competition and pricing trends, plus my view on which preparation is worth doing before you list. It's free and there's no obligation. Request yours here.

How fast do you respond?

Usually the same day. Texting is the fastest way to reach me.
